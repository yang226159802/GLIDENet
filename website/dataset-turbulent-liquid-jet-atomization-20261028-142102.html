Turbulent liquid jet atomization

A dense cylindrical turbulent liquid jet is continuously injected into a quiescent lighter phase

Description

This dataset simulates the three-dimensional atomisation of a pulsed liquid jet injected into initially stationary gas. Inlet velocity oscillations promote interfacial instabilities, jet breakup, and droplet formation. The computational domain measures 3 × 3 × 3, extending from x = 0 to 3 and from −1.5 to 1.5 in both transverse directions. The jet has a radius of 1/12 and an initial length of 0.025. Its mean inlet velocity is 1, with a sinusoidal fluctuation of amplitude 0.05 and period 0.1; the liquid-to-gas density ratio is 27.84. The simulation is performed with Basilisk, which solves the incompressible two-phase Navier-Stokes equations, tracks the liquid-gas interface using a volume-of-fluid method, and includes surface tension. It starts from a 64³ grid and uses adaptive refinement up to level 11, with a velocity error threshold of 0.1. Snapshots are recorded every 0.1 simulation time units through 3.7. Each configuration varies the jet Reynolds number or Weber number, enabling comparison of their effects on breakup and droplet formation.
